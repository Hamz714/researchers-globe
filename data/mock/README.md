# Mock data — NOT REAL, do not cite

Everything in this folder exists so nobody is blocked before the real data lands.

- `mines_mock.csv`: 20 well-known iron ore mines. Names and owners are real but
  **coordinates are approximate, ownership shares and export ports are illustrative**,
  and capacity is deliberately blank. Replace with the Global Iron Ore Mines Tracker.
- `ports_mock.csv`, `trade_mock.csv`, `countries_mock.csv`: illustrative values,
  `source_url = MOCK`. Trade shares are invented round numbers.
- `mock_result.json`: a hand-written `/query` response in the exact contract shape.
  Article URLs point to example.com on purpose.
